1. Course Registration

  • A course booking is confirmed after the applicable payment has been successfully received.
  • Before payment, students should review the course name, eligibility, date, timing, venue and other information provided for the class.
  • Students should pay only after ensuring that they are comfortable with the announced date, timing and venue.
  • Course payments are for the specific educational/service offering selected.

2. Course Dates and Timings

  • Course dates and timings are solely decided by LIGHTWORKERS BRAHAMAPUTRA TRUST.
  • Dates and timings are informed before payment. Students should pay only if they are able to attend according to the announced schedule.

  • If a class date is changed, the payment remains applicable to the rescheduled class.

3. Venue and Delivery

Classes may be conducted at the Trust's registered address or at another venue arranged or rented by the Trust. Classes may also be conducted in association with another organisation affiliated with, associated with, or working with the Trust.

4. Donations

  • Donations are voluntary contributions to LIGHTWORKERS BRAHAMAPUTRA TRUST.
  • A donation is not a purchase of a course, product or other service unless expressly stated otherwise.
  • Donors should verify payment details before completing a donation.

5. Website Use

  • Users should provide accurate information when requested for registration, communication or payment purposes.
  • Users must not misuse, interfere with or attempt to disrupt the website or payment functionality.
  • Course descriptions, schedules and other website information may be updated from time to time.

Cancellation and Refund

1. Course Payments

Once a course has been paid for, the booking cannot be cancelled by the student and there will be no refund.

  • Students should check the course date, timing, eligibility and venue before payment.
  • Payment should be made only when the student is satisfied with the announced schedule and is willing to attend.
  • Failure to attend a class does not make the payment refundable.
  • Personal scheduling conflicts, travel difficulties or change of mind do not create an entitlement to a refund.

2. Change of Class Date

The Trust may change the date or timing of a class. The original payment remains applicable to the rescheduled class. A change in class date does not by itself create a right to a refund.

3. Donations

Donations are voluntary contributions and are not course purchases. Once a donation has been successfully completed, it should be treated as a completed contribution. Any request concerning a donation will be considered by the Trust based on the circumstances and applicable requirements.

4. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you believe that a payment was duplicated or made incorrectly, contact the Trust as soon as possible with the transaction details so that the payment can be reviewed and reconciled.

Shipping and Exchange

1. Nature of the Courses

The courses covered by these policies are services and are not physical products that are shipped to the customer.

2. Course Delivery

Classes are delivered at the Trust's registered address or at another venue rented or arranged by the Trust. Classes may also be conducted in association with another organisation affiliated with, associated with, or working with the Trust.

3. Shipping

No physical shipping is applicable to a course registration. The payment confirms registration for the applicable service, subject to the course terms and announced schedule.

4. Exchange

Course registrations are not exchangeable for physical products, another course or another service unless the Trust expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

5. Rescheduled Classes

If the Trust changes the date of a class, the existing registration/payment remains applicable to the rescheduled class. Students will be informed of the revised schedule.

6. Donations

Donations do not involve shipping or exchange of physical goods.
