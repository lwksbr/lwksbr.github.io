Day 6 · 16 October Maa Durga's Nine-Day Journey
Today's Inner Practice

Attachment → Acceptance, trust and emotional freedom

Understanding the Weakness

Attachment becomes limiting when our peace depends entirely on another person's behaviour, a particular outcome or the fulfilment of our expectations. It may appear as possessiveness, overthinking, excessive control or difficulty accepting change. Maa Katyayani represents courage and decisive action, reminding us to distinguish what we can change from what we must accept.

Purification Process

  1. Recognise: Identify the person, outcome or expectation you are trying to control.
  2. Understand: Ask whether your expectations are realistic and whether they respect another person's freedom.
  3. Release: Accept that you cannot control every decision, response or circumstance.
  4. Replace: Focus on your own choices, communicate honestly and maintain healthy boundaries.
Pranic Healing perspective: Practise observing thoughts and expectations with awareness. Acceptance does not mean passivity; it means acting wisely where you have influence.

Journal Reflection

  • What am I holding on to out of fear?
  • Am I confusing love with control?
  • What can I change, and what must I accept?
Today's Action

Release one unrealistic expectation and take one constructive step within your control.

Today's Affirmation

“I act with wisdom and courage. I respect others' freedom, accept life's changes and release what I cannot control.”
