Day 8 · 18 October Maa Durga's Nine-Day Journey
Today's Inner Practice

Resentment and unforgiveness → Forgiveness, peace and inner purity

Understanding the Weakness

Resentment can remain within us long after a painful experience has passed. We may repeatedly revisit an argument, blame someone, replay a betrayal or punish ourselves for past decisions. Forgiveness is not forgetting what happened, excusing harmful behaviour or allowing someone to hurt us again. It is the process of loosening the hold that resentment has on our inner life. Maa Mahagauri symbolises purity, peace and serenity.

Purification Process

  1. Recognise: Identify an unresolved hurt or regret that continues to disturb your peace.
  2. Understand: Acknowledge what happened and how it affected you, without minimising your experience.
  3. Release: Begin letting go of repetitive blame and self-punishment. Allow the process to take the time it needs.
  4. Replace: Cultivate compassion, self-respect, acceptance and healthy boundaries.
Pranic Healing perspective: Allow emotional processing to happen at its own pace. Forgiveness does not require reconciliation or abandoning healthy boundaries.

Journal Reflection

  • What hurt am I still carrying?
  • What do I need to acknowledge or learn from this experience?
  • What boundary or change would help me move forward peacefully?
Today's Action

Write a private letter expressing your feelings. You do not need to send it. End by identifying what you wish to release and what you wish to protect going forward.

Today's Affirmation

“I cleanse my heart of resentment, one step at a time. I forgive where I am ready, honour my boundaries and make space for peace.”

Closing Prayer to the Divine Mother

Divine Mother, through Your nine sacred forms, guide me towards inner purification. Give me the awareness to recognise my weaknesses without judgment, the courage to face my fears, the patience to transform my reactions, and the wisdom to release thoughts and habits that no longer serve my highest good. May anger become patience, fear become courage, greed become contentment, jealousy become appreciation, ego become humility, attachment become acceptance, negativity become discipline, resentment become forgiveness, and selfishness become selfless service. May my thoughts become clearer, my emotions more balanced, my communication more compassionate and my actions more meaningful. May I carry the blessings of Navratri into my daily life and become a better human being, a source of harmony in my relationships and an instrument of peace, love and service to all. Jai Mata Di! Jai Maa Durga! 🌺🙏
