Navratri 9-Day Inner Purification Programme

A nine-day journey to transform our weaknesses into virtues through self-awareness, meditation and conscious action.

About the Programme

Worshipping the Divine Mother Within — From Weakness to Virtue

A guided journey of self-awareness, emotional cleansing, meditation and conscious transformation, inspired by the nine forms of Maa Durga and the principles of Pranic Healing.

Navratri offers an opportunity to go beyond external rituals and begin an inner journey. Each day, take inspiration from one form of Divine Shakti while honestly examining a weakness, understanding its roots, and replacing it with a higher virtue.

The purpose is not to condemn ourselves for our weaknesses, but to become aware of them, release unhelpful patterns and consciously cultivate our divine qualities.

Gentle reminder: The weakness-to-virtue pairings are suggested practices for spiritual self-reflection, not fixed traditional associations of each Goddess.
How to Practise Each Day

Set aside approximately 25–35 minutes daily. Choose a quiet place and approach the practice with sincerity and kindness towards yourself.

  1. Invoke the Divine Mother: Light a diya if appropriate, offer a flower, chant the Goddess's name and set an intention for inner transformation.
  2. Calm the mind — 5 minutes: Breathe gently and observe your thoughts and emotions without suppressing them or judging yourself.
  3. Identify the weakness — 5–7 minutes: Reflect on the triggers, beliefs, fears or expectations that may contribute to the pattern.
  4. Visualise the virtue — 5 minutes: Imagine yourself responding to a challenging situation with the virtue of the day. Choose a practical, compassionate response.
  5. Commit to action — 5–10 minutes: Choose one specific behaviour to practise today and review your progress before sleeping.
