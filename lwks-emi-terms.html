Terms & Conditions of Installment Agreement

Please read the following terms carefully before choosing an installment payment facility.

A Note of Welcome: At M/S Lightworkers Brahamaputra, our relationship with you is built on trust, transparency, and mutual respect. This installment facility is designed to help you access our products comfortably over time.

1. EMI Eligiblility

EMI facility can be availed only when at least 50% of the total dues have been cleared. The remaining eligible amount may then be considered for payment through the applicable EMI option, subject to the terms and conditions of the EMI facility.

2. Calculation Structure & Flexible Timelines

To accommodate different budgeting needs, we offer installment timelines ranging from three (3) months. To help cover administrative management, a one-time service charge is integrated based on the timeline you choose:

  • Base Program (3 Months): A 5% service charge is added to the product price.
  • Extended Program (4 Months Onwards): Each additional month adds a modest 1% to the base charge.
    Example: 4 Months = 6% | 5 Months = 7% | 6 Months = 8%
  • Rounding Rule: Each monthly payment is rounded up to the nearest whole Rupee. The final total will adjust to reflect this.

3. Passing of Ownership

Ownership of the product transfers directly to you upon delivery. You become the official owner right from day one, with the understanding that you will honor the remaining installment schedule in good faith.

4. Payment Schedule and Clear Timelines

All monthly payments are systematically due on the 1st day of each subsequent calendar month following your purchase.

5. Keeping Things Simple (Single-Account Policy)

To ensure our customers do not take on a heavier financial load than comfortable, we follow a strict one-installment-plan-at-a-time policy. New installment options require the previous balance to be completely cleared first.

6. Keeping Your Account in Good Standing

If a payment on the 1st is missed due to an insufficient account balance, the following structural guidelines apply to help protect our shared community ecosystem:

  • First Month: A 5% fee calculated on that specific monthly payment will be added. We kindly ask that you clear this missed payment along with its 5% fee within 30 days.
  • Further Delays: If an amount remains unresolved beyond 30 days, an additional tracking fee of 3% per month will apply, starting after the 1st month of delay, to the overdue balance until it is brought up to date.
  • Future Eligibility: Our program relies entirely on consistent trust. If an installment is missed, your eligibility to purchase items through future installment programs will be paused permanently.

7. Return and Refund Policy

Because we plan our inventory to support this program, all sales made under an installment plan are considered final. Products cannot be returned or refunded unless authorized at the sole discretion of the company.
